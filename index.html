ANAMNESIS MUNDI

A source-based chronological map of ancient cosmologies and humanity’s search for origins.

The present chronology begins with the Sethian account preserved in The Secret Book of John, which provides its initial narrative framework. Each stage is grounded in identified primary sources, while related texts, variant traditions, and cross-cultural parallels are presented as distinct layers rather than merged into a single system.

As Anamnesis Mundi grows, further ancient texts, manuscript traditions, source research, and comparative pathways will be added.
