The First Unfolding

BARBELO

In the Secret Book of John, Barbelo emerges in immediate relation to the supreme Invisible Spirit. She is the first Thought or Forethought, the image of the Invisible Spirit, the first power and the first to come forth.

Position in the chronology

The first articulated divine reality

Barbelo marks the first unfolding of the divine realm. When the Monad looks into its own light and reflects upon itself, this self-awareness is presented as the supreme feminine divine principle. Barbelo becomes the primordial Thought, the universal womb and the locus through which the higher spiritual realm begins to unfold.

Thematic explorer

Barbelo’s attendant aeons

Barbelo requests and receives four initial divine attributes from the Invisible Spirit. The source presents them as distinct aeonic powers associated with the first unfolding of the divine fullness.

I Aeonic power

Foreknowledge

At Barbelo’s request, Foreknowledge comes forth from the Invisible Spirit and stands beside Forethought.

II Aeonic power

Incorruptibility

Barbelo requests Incorruptibility, which comes forth as a distinct power and joins the unfolding divine fullness.

III Aeonic power

Eternal Life

Eternal Life comes forth at Barbelo’s request and takes its place within the initial aeonic complex surrounding her.

IV Aeonic power

Truth

Truth is the fourth power requested by Barbelo and completes this sequence of attendant aeonic realities.

Textual note

The aeonic complex around Barbelo

Their enumeration contributes to the complex described as an androgynous pentad and, in its paired aspect, a decad. Truth here belongs to this initial complex and is not merged with the later aeon named Truth in Armozel’s triad.
