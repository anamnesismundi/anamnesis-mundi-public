Pleroma · Sequence 3/3

Sophia’s Independent Generation

The rupture within the Pleroma

In The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), one of the central works of the Nag Hammadi Library, the Pleroma is the divine Fullness, the ordered realm unfolding from the Invisible Spirit. Sophia, whose name means Wisdom, belongs to the final movement of this divine order. Within the Pleroma, she undertakes an independent act of generation.

After this independent act brings forth Yaldabaoth, Sophia perceives a change in her own light. Its radiance has diminished, and she understands the deficiency that has entered her condition. She begins to move to and fro. John, the son of Zebedee, asks what this movement means, and the revealer explains that Sophia has seen what has come to pass and has entered repentance. Her movement gives visible form to her inner displacement as Wisdom seeks restoration within the divine order.

Her supplication is heard by the whole Fullness. A blessing descends upon her, and the Holy Spirit pours upon her from the Pleroma. Sophia is raised above the being she has produced, while the restoration of the deficiency prepares her eventual return to her own aeon.
