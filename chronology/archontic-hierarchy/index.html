The Archontic Heavens · Sequence 2/2

The Emergence of the Archons

Subordinate rulers and the expansion of authority

In The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), the archontic hierarchy unfolds through twelve subordinate authorities and a distinct group of seven powers. Seven rulers are appointed over the heavens and five over the abyss, while the powers generate further angels, bringing their stated total to 365.

Yaldabaoth unites the seven powers with the authorities through his thought and gives each power a name. Yet these powers bear two names: those bestowed by their maker possess power, while those given after the glory above will bring about their destruction and the loss of their power.

The archontic order is organized after the pattern of the first aeons, although its maker has never seen the incorruptible beings. This pattern comes through the power received from Sophia, allowing the lower order to take shape after the divine realm above.
