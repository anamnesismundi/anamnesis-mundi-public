The Emergence of the Demiurge

Yaldabaoth and the beginning of the lower order

In The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), one of the central works of the Nag Hammadi Library, Yaldabaoth comes forth from Sophia’s solitary act of generation. Unlike the beings of the Pleroma, he is born outside the harmony of the divine Fullness. Sophia names him Yaldabaoth and casts him beyond the realm of the immortals, enclosing him within a luminous cloud and placing a throne there, hidden from the beings above.

Yaldabaoth bears the title of Chief Ruler and carries within himself a great power received from his Mother. Yet he does not know the source from which that power has come. He cannot behold the divine realm above him, nor recognize the Fullness from which his own existence ultimately descends. He took control and created for himself other aeons with luminous fire, which still exists.

Beyond the Pleroma, another order now begins to arise. Its ruler possesses power without knowledge of its origin, light without knowledge of the greater light from which it came. From this blindness a new realm takes form, separated from the Fullness yet unknowingly dependent upon it.
