The Emergence of the Demiurge

Yaldabaoth and the beginning of the lower order

In The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), one of the central works of the Nag Hammadi Library, Yaldabaoth appears after Sophia’s independent act of generation. The offspring Sophia has brought forth is unlike the beings of the Pleroma. She names him Yaldabaoth, then removes him from their midst, enclosing him in a luminous cloud and setting a throne there so that the beings above will not see him.

Yaldabaoth bears the title of Chief Ruler and great power from his Mother. Yet he does not know the source of that power, nor the divine realm from which he came. He turns away from the place of his birth and makes an aeon for himself, blazing with luminous fire.

Beyond the Pleroma, a new realm begins to take shape around him, ruled by one who cannot see the source of his own power.
