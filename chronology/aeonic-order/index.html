The Aeonic Order

Four Great Luminaries · Twelve Aeons · Spiritual Generations

The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), one of the central works of the Nag Hammadi Library, tells of the Pleroma, the divine Fullness. In this passage, the revelation unfolds from Autogenes, the Self-Generated Child, toward the great lights, the aeons, and the heavenly Human.

From the light of Autogenes (Christ), and through the gift of the Invisible Spirit, the four great lights come forth. With each light, the text names three aeons, opening the fullness of their divine order.

Then the revelation turns to the perfect Human. Through the foreknowledge of the perfect Mind and the will of the Invisible Spirit and Autogenes, he appears as the first revelation and truth. The Spirit calls him Pigera-Adamas, also rendered Geradamas, and gives him spiritual, invincible power.

Seth, the Seed of Seth, the souls of the saints, and those who come to repentance after first being ignorant of the Fullness are also placed within this divine order. Thus, before the story turns to Sophia’s independent generation, the heavenly Human and the line of Seth already have their place in the Pleroma.
