Pleroma · Sequence 2/3

The Aeonic Order

Four Great Luminaries · Twelve Aeons · Spiritual Generations

The Secret Book of John (The Apocryphon of John), one of the central works of the Nag Hammadi Library, reveals the Pleroma as the divine Fullness in which the first orders of being come forth. From Autogenes, the Self-Generated Child, the unfolding continues into the great lights, the aeons, and at last the heavenly Human, each arising within an order whose source remains the Invisible Spirit.

From the light of Autogenes, Christ, and through the gift of the Invisible Spirit, the four great luminaries come forth. Around each of them gather three aeons, forming the twelvefold order through which the divine Fullness takes shape. Light opens into light, and what first appeared in the Child expands into a living order of powers and eternal realms.

Within this luminous order appears the perfect Human. Through the foreknowledge of the perfect Mind, and by the will of the Invisible Spirit and Autogenes, he comes forth as the first revelation and truth. The Spirit names him Pigera-Adamas, also rendered Geradamas, and endows him with a spiritual power that cannot be overcome. Humanity thus appears first not in the world below, but within the divine Fullness, as an image belonging to the realm of light.

From the heavenly Human the unfolding continues through Seth, the Seed of Seth, the souls of the saints, and those who, having once lived in ignorance of the Fullness, awaken and turn toward it. Each finds a place within the order of the Pleroma. Before Sophia acts apart from her consort and the harmony of the Fullness is disturbed, the lineage of the heavenly Human has already been established among the realms of light.
